About Me

Why I Build Things

Most of my projects start with a simple question:

"Why is this harder than it should be?"

Sometimes it's a confusing engineering topic. Sometimes it's a repetitive workflow. Sometimes it's my own inability to stay focused.

That curiosity has led me to build everything from AI orchestration systems and educational tools to productivity experiments and creative AI applications. I'm currently studying Computer Engineering at SSASIT, Surat, while freelancing, exploring new technologies, and building products that solve problems I personally experience.

My biggest interest isn't automation itself. It's understanding how people think, learn, get distracted, and interact with technology.

That's why many of my projects combine AI, psychology, visual learning, and system design rather than focusing on software alone.
